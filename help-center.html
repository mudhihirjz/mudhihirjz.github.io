Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ticket?
To book a ticket, go to the "Book Ticket" page, search for your trip, select your preferred bus, choose your seat, enter passenger details, make payment, and you'll receive your e-ticket instantly.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept M-PESA, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Visa/Mastercard, and Bank Transfer. All payments are secure and encrypted.
How do I cancel my booking?
You can cancel your booking from the "My Bookings" page. Find your booking and click the "Cancel" button. Refunds are processed within 24-48 hours.
How do I get my e-ticket?
Your e-ticket is generated immediately after payment confirmation. You can download it from the confirmation page, or find it in "My Bookings" under the booking details.
What if I miss my bus?
If you miss your bus, please contact our support team immediately at +255 712 345 678. We'll do our best to help you find an alternative trip.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes, refunds are available for cancellations made at least 24 hours before departure. A small cancellation fee may apply. Contact our support team for more details.
