About DE TRAVELS

Your trusted partner for comfortable and safe bus travel across Tanzania.

Who We Are

DE TRAVELS is a leading bus transport company in Tanzania, committed to providing safe, reliable, and comfortable travel experiences. With a fleet of modern buses and a team of professional staff, we connect major cities across the country.

Founded in 2015, we have grown to become a trusted name in the transport industry, serving over 200+ happy customers and operating 100+ buses across Tanzania.

Our mission is to make travel accessible, affordable, and enjoyable for everyone. Whether you're traveling for business or leisure, we ensure your journey is smooth and memorable.
