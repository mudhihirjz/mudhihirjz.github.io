Frequently Asked Questions
How do I place an order?
Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order is placed.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery takes 2-5 business days depending on your location. We deliver nationwide with tracking provided.
Can I return a product?
Yes, we accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Please contact our support team for assistance.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Mobile Money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money), bank transfers, and cash on delivery.
Do you offer warranty on products?
Yes, all our products come with a manufacturer's warranty. Contact us for warranty claims.
How can I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via SMS and email to monitor your delivery.
